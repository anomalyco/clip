Clip now updates itself.

- **Updates.** Clip checks for a new version in the background and downloads it. Choose **Restart to Update** in the menu bar (or just quit) to switch to it. **Check for Updates…** checks right away.
- **⌃ ⌃ records again.** In 0.6, double-tapping Control sometimes opened Clip's window instead of recording. It now always records, and only shows the permissions step when macOS really refuses.
- **Whole screen by default.** ⌃ ⌃ records the whole screen at once. Turn on **Choose what to record** on the home window to pick a window or area first.
- **Pick the shot afterwards.** The review's **Shot** row frames the whole desktop, one window (following it as it moves), or whichever window was in front.
