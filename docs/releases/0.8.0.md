A cleaner review, and exports drawn by Clip itself.

- **A simpler review.** The recording and its timeline, with **Copy ⏎**, **GIF ⇧⏎** and **Discard Esc** under them, and a narrow sidebar of what changes from clip to clip: Shot, Background, Crop, Zoom on clicks, Speed up idle and Speed. Each control shows its key. Quality, frame rate and export size live on the home window.
- **What you preview is what you export.** Clip now draws every frame of an export itself on the GPU, the same way for the preview: backgrounds, rounded corners, shadows and zoom. Colours stay truer to the recording, and GIFs keep the clip's length exactly.
- **No more black corners** around window recordings on a background, even zoomed in.
- **Clearer errors.** A recording that's gone or damaged, or a clips folder Clip can't write to, now says so plainly instead of failing silently.
